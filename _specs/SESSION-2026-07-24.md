# Session log — 0.6.4.9 → 0.6.5.9

Everything since the resource tracker was fixed. Versions are the shipped tags on `main`
(Cloudflare auto-deploys each push).

## 1 · Resources, riders and Extra Attack

**0.6.4.9 — spell resources actually spend.** Free-cast spells and levelled slots were not
ticking down. `consumeActionResourcesOnCommit` now branches three ways — free cast → named
resource, levelled spell → slot, named `slotCost` → named resource — and matching is
substring-based so a label like "1st-level slots" still resolves. Manual tick-down stays as
a fallback, gated to pool / counter / spellSlot / freeCast so it can't corrupt other rows.
Monster initiative also became rollable mid-combat instead of setup-only.

A resource-orphan audit found **19 pools with no action spending them**. 20 class actions
were wired to their pools; 4 remain deliberately unwired (Relentless Endurance is a trigger,
Eldritch Cannon HP and Replicated Items are trackers, and Lyrielle's L2 slots have no L2
spell yet).

**0.6.5.0 — Extra Attack repeats the same weapon.** `completeCommittedRoll` always retired
the readied key, so a PC could not use the same weapon for their second attack without a
reset. It now retires the key only when the multiattack budget is actually spent.

**0.6.5.1 — riders clear at End Combat.** Added `weaponBuffAttack` alongside the existing
`weaponBuffDamage`, so an armed attack-side rider (Archery, Sacred Weapon) persists correctly.
Every rider — Rage, Sacred Weapon, Hunter's Mark and the additive/bond riders — now clears on
the `FDMC_COMBAT_END_CHANNEL` broadcast rather than lingering into the next fight.

## 2 · The monster combat card

Four asks against a target mockup; three are done.

**0.6.5.2 — expandable text.** Action and trait text was clipping mid-sentence with no way to
open it.

**0.6.5.3 — damage dice coloured by type.** New `core/constants/damageTypeVisuals.ts`: a
type → {colour, icon} map plus `splitTypedDamage(damage, text)`, which pairs each parenthetical
die in the rules text with the type word following it and falls back to a single plain chip when
it can't parse. 27 of 36 library actions colour correctly; the other 9 fall back cleanly.

**0.6.5.4 — spell slots, and Multiattack removed.**
- Casters carry structured `stats.spellSlots: [{level, max}]`, threaded through the runtime into
  each instance. Slot-costed actions tag `spellSlotLevel`. The card shows per-level trackers and a
  remaining-slot pill that decrements on use. Frozen Sentinel 1st(4)/2nd(3)/3rd(2); Frost-Weaver
  1st(3)/2nd(2)/3rd(2)/4th(1). Reset Turn clears the per-turn economy but **preserves spent slots**.
- All 13 `"Multiattack"` action rows deleted. The attack budget now comes from
  `stats.attacksPerTurn` (10 creatures tagged, Fortbreaker Reaver 3) and drives an `ActionBudget`
  dot row. An attack spends one step; a non-attack main action spends the whole budget — PC economy.

> A first migration script silently dropped the attack count on 10 Act 2 creatures whose `stats`
> block spans multiple lines. Caught by re-running the audit, reverted via git, and rewritten to
> track the line carrying `maxHp:`. Second run: 13 removed, 10 tagged, 0 leftover.

**Still open:** the header row (AC / HP-state / Speed / State cells). The economy dots it calls
for already exist.

## 4 · Companions could not act — root cause

Faelar had been re-tagged as a `player` because the companion tag "wasn't allowing her to do
actions."

`buildCombatants` correctly pulls an **owned** companion out of the top-level initiative order and
nests it under its owner, and the tracker did draw the indented sub-row. But that row's name was a
plain `<span>`, while every other row is a `<button>` wired to `onSelectCombatant`. **There was no
way to click her**, so her card never opened and she could never spend an action — which is why
"next turn" looked like it skipped her. Only *orphaned* (ownerless) companions were selectable,
because those fall through to the standalone list. Setting an owner is what broke her.

**0.6.5.6** — the companion name is now a button, and the row lights up in the owner's seat colour
while the owner is active. The `isActiveTurn` gate in `App.tsx` was always correct; it never got the
chance. Faelar can go back to `kind: "companion"`.

## 5 · Faelar — Beast of the Land

**0.6.5.6 / .7 / .8.** Upgraded from the pre-BotL wolf placeholder. Almost nothing on a Primal
Companion stat block is its own — AC, HP, to-hit, damage, saves and checks all read off the ranger —
so the file takes **three inputs** and computes the rest. A level-up is one line.

```
RANGER_LEVEL = 5 · RANGER_PROFICIENCY_BONUS = 3 · RANGER_WIS_MOD = 2
```

| | formula | value |
|---|---|---|
| AC | 13 + ranger's **WIS** mod | 15 |
| HP | **5 + 5 × ranger level** | 30 |
| To hit | ranger's spell attack mod (PB + WIS) | +5 |
| Damage | 1d8 + 2 + ranger's WIS mod | 1d8+4 |
| Saves | ability mod + PB, **all six** (Primal Bond) | +5/+5/+5/+2/+5/+3 |

Two corrections along the way: AC is keyed to Wisdom, not the beast's Dexterity; and HP is
`5 + 5 × level`, not a flat 30 and not `5 × level`. The HP formula also re-pinned Lyrielle at
**level 5**, confirmed three ways — PB 3 (levels 5–8), HP 30, and 5 Hit Dice.

Values are computed rather than written as `@vars`, because `@WIS`/`@PROF` resolve against the
**beast**, not the ranger. They are numerically identical today, which is exactly the coincidence
that would break silently on her next ASI.

> The bundled `lyrielle.ts` still says level 2 and is stale relative to the live room.

## 6 · Bonds v13

`broken_chain_campaign_bonds_v13.html` supersedes v12.

**Crit rule (global).** Bond dice never crit — on a critical hit a bond die is rolled once and added
flat. The attack roll itself crits normally, *including a companion's stat block attack*; only the
bond's own dice are excluded. So Beast's Strike and its charge 1d6 double; Pack Instinct's additive
die (+1d4 Metamorphosis → +1d6 Apex → +1d8 Unbroken) never does. Faelar's stale `crit` of
`2d8+2+@WIS` was corrected to `2d8+4` — dice double, the flat modifier does not.

**Pack Instinct II Realized** — "your companion moves up to its full speed and **uses its stat block
attack action**." So Bonded Strike is the true Beast's Strike at `1d8+4`, including Lyrielle's
Wisdom. The sheet's "do not add Faelar's Wisdom" line was superseded v12 text.

> I briefly reverted this as an unauthorised buff after an audit flagged that wording. The wording
> was simply stale. General progression: early stages grow the die only; from Metamorphosis the
> chosen line adds PB; the personal modifier appears only at Unbroken.
